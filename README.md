# Adidas-Sales-Analysis-2020-2021
An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard analysing Adidas sales performance, profitability, product categories, regional performance, retailers, and sales channels for 2020–2021.
Project Overview
This project focuses on analysing Adidas sales performance for 2020 and 2021 using an interactive Excel dashboard. The analysis evaluates overall sales, operating profit, product performance, regional performance, sales channels, and retailer contributions to identify business trends and opportunities for improvement.
The dashboard provides a visual representation of key business performance indicators, enabling stakeholders to monitor sales activities, evaluate profitability, and make data-driven business decisions.
Project Objectives
The major objectives of this analysis are to:
•	Evaluate Adidas' overall sales and operating profit.
•	Analyse sales performance across different geographical regions.
•	Identify top-performing and low-performing product categories.
•	Examine monthly and yearly sales trends.
•	Compare sales performance across different distribution channels.
•	Assess retailer contributions to total sales and operating profit.
•	Identify business opportunities for improving revenue and profitability.

Dashboard Visualisations
The dashboard contains the following analytical sections:
1. Regional Performance
Evaluates total sales and operating profit across the West, Northeast, Southeast, South, and Midwest regions to identify geographical performance differences.
2. Product Performance
Compares sales and operating profit across six product categories, including Men's Apparel, Men's Athletic Footwear, Men's Street Footwear, Women's Apparel, Women's Athletic Footwear, and Women's Street Footwear.
3. Monthly and Yearly Trends
Examines monthly sales patterns from January to December to identify fluctuations, growth periods, and seasonal trends.
4. Channel Performance
Analyses the contribution of three sales channels: In-store, Online, and Outlet.
5. Retailer Contribution
Compares sales and operating profit generated by major retailers, including Walmart, Amazon, Kohl's, Sports Direct, Foot Locker, and West Gear.
6. Business Opportunity
Highlights regional sales and operating profit comparisons to identify geographical markets with potential for improved business performance.
Key Business Insights
•	The West region demonstrates strong sales performance compared with other geographical regions.
•	Product categories contribute differently to overall sales and operating profit, highlighting opportunities for product-level performance improvement.
•	Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year, suggesting variations in customer demand and possible seasonal purchasing patterns.
•	Adidas generates sales through multiple distribution channels, providing opportunities to strengthen its market reach.
•	Retailer contributions vary, indicating differences in sales performance across distribution partners.
•	Regional comparisons reveal opportunities to improve performance in areas with lower sales contributions.
Business Recommendations
1.	Regional Expansion: Strengthen marketing and distribution strategies in regions with lower sales performance while maintaining strong-performing markets.
2.	Product Strategy: Prioritise high-performing product categories and review the pricing, marketing, and inventory strategies of weaker categories.
3.	Seasonal Marketing: Use monthly sales trends to plan promotional campaigns and inventory levels around periods of higher customer demand.
4.	Channel Optimisation: Strengthen online sales while maintaining effective in-store and outlet distribution strategies.
5.	Retailer Management: Build stronger partnerships with high-performing retailers and develop improvement strategies for retailers generating lower sales.
6.	Profitability Monitoring: Monitor operating profit alongside revenue to ensure that sales growth translates into sustainable business profitability.
